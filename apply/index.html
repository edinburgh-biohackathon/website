Your application

Tell us about yourself, your interests and skills, and upload your CV. Your answers help us form balanced teams and make the right arrangements for the event. You can save and come back, and edit after submitting until applications close.

Before you start
  1. Add teammates if you already know who you'd like to work with. Otherwise there is an in-person networking session before the hackathon.
  2. Challenge preferences are for information only. Sponsors present all challenges at the opening ceremony, and you choose then.
  3. Teams (4–6 people) and challenges are confirmed on the day.
  4. Please don't use generative AI for your application. Short answers and bullet points are best.
  5. During the hackathon itself, generative AI is welcome.
